When I say mini rack I mean 10 inch rack. I've got loads of kit just lying on the fllor in the attic waiting to be todden on and so putting it into something has been a plan for a while. So I searched about and found some resources from [Jeff Geerling](https://mini-rack.jeffgeerling.com/#3d-printable-systems) and ended up buying a 3D printed [LabRax](https://the-diy-life.com/introducing-lab-rax-a-3d-printable-modular-10-rack-system/) clone because it was dirt cheap and an easy way to test out configuration before needing to stump up for an expensive metal one (if its needed at all)

Once it starts to take shape I'll update this post but for now its an empty rack, so not much to add other than to say, that this stuff is awesome. Can't believe I haven't gone down this rabbit hole before
